# Customer_Behavior_Analysis

A Data Analytics project focused on understanding customer behavior, purchasing patterns, sales trends, and customer segmentation using **Python, SQL, and Power BI**. The project transforms raw customer and transaction data into actionable business insights through data cleaning, exploratory analysis, SQL queries, and interactive dashboards.

> This project is created for learning, portfolio, and demonstration purposes.

## Project Overview

The **Customer Behavior Analysis** project analyzes customer and transaction data to identify:

* Customer purchasing patterns
* Sales and revenue trends
* High-value and low-value customers
* Customer segmentation
* Product/category performance
* Repeat purchase behavior
* Customer spending patterns
* Key business performance indicators (KPIs)

The analysis combines **Python for data cleaning and exploratory data analysis, SQL for business-focused data analysis, and Power BI for interactive visualization and reporting**.

## Problems Solved

### 1. Understanding Customer Behavior

Analyzes customer purchase history to identify patterns in spending, purchase frequency, and customer engagement.

### 2. Identifying High-Value Customers

Uses customer-level metrics such as total spending, purchase frequency, and average order value to identify valuable customers.

### 3. Analyzing Sales Performance

Examines revenue, orders, quantity sold, and sales trends across different time periods, products, and customer segments.

### 4. Customer Segmentation

Groups customers based on their purchasing behavior and spending patterns to support targeted marketing and business strategies.

### 5. Identifying Business Trends

Uses historical transaction data to identify monthly and yearly sales trends, popular products, and changes in customer purchasing behavior.

### 6. Interactive Business Reporting

Creates an interactive Power BI dashboard that allows users to filter and explore customer, sales, product, and revenue insights.
